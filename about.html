About Mnemonic Pay

A simple way to share money with family

Mnemonic Pay was created to make it easy for family members to share money without the complexity of traditional banking apps. It's like a digital version of handing someone cash, but with the convenience of sending it directly to their debit card.

Our system uses memorable 4-word phrases instead of long account numbers or complicated payment links. These phrases are easy to write down, share verbally, or send in a message.

Why use Mnemonic Pay?
Simple & Memorable

4-word phrases are easier to remember and share than 16-digit numbers or complex links.

No Account Required

Recipients don't need to create an account or download an app to receive money.

Instant Transfers

Money is sent directly to the recipient's debit card within seconds.

Secure & Private

Each phrase can only be used once and all payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Perfect for:
  • Sending money to family members who aren't tech-savvy
  • Giving cash gifts for birthdays or holidays
  • Helping out with emergency expenses
  • Sharing money when you can't meet in person
